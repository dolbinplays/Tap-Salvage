# Scrap Shelter Lite Backlog

This backlog captures promising ideas that should not distract from proving the tap-salvage MVP loop.

## Future Feature: Press Your Junk

An original bonus-risk mini game inspired by the feeling of old TV game-show risk rounds, adapted to the salvage fantasy.

### Core Concept
- Player wagers a small bundle of inventory items.
- A junkyard board rapidly highlights prize spaces.
- Player presses a stop button to claim the highlighted result.
- Player can bank winnings or press again.
- Bad spaces can bust the round and steal the wager.

### Possible Rewards
- Rare materials.
- Cosmetic scraps.
- Room skin parts.
- Tool decals.
- Upgrade coupons.
- Salvage permits or temporary boosts.

### Design Guardrails
- Treat this as a later retention feature, not core MVP.
- Keep presentation original: scrapyard workbench, busted machine, unstable recycler, or similar.
- Avoid direct use of protected game-show terminology, art style, characters, or exact board presentation.
- The bad result should be funny and thematic, but not so punishing that players feel robbed.
- Rewarded ad option, if tested later: one extra safe press or protect half the wager.

### Suggested Roadmap Slot
Lite 2C, after the core salvage loop, upgrades, retention hooks, and rewarded-ad placements feel stable.
